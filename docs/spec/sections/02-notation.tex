\section{Notation}
\label{sec:notation}

This section gathers every symbol used in the math that follows, in one
table for reference.

All NAV-denominated quantities carry WAD precision ($10^{18}$). Senior
tranche shares and BPT amounts are 18-decimal. Quote-asset units track the
underlying token's decimals (e.g.\ $10^6$ for USDC).

  \midrule
  $\STraw$           & Raw NAV of the senior tranche's own assets                                       & NAV \\

  \midrule

  $E$                & Error function, $E(x) = T(x) - x$; bisection target                              & function \\

  $f$                & Junior tranche's share of the pool, $f = \mathrm{juniorBPT} / \mathrm{totalBPTSupply}$ & dimensionless \\
